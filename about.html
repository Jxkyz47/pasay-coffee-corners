About The Guide.

A personal collection of places, cups, and little moments.

The Person Behind The Guide

Hi, I'm Jefferson.

I have always found something comforting about coffee shops—the soft hum of conversations, the smell of freshly brewed coffee, and the feeling of having somewhere to slow down.

I created Pasay Coffee Corners as a way to share some of the places that I find interesting and worth visiting. Whether you are looking for somewhere to study, meet friends, take pictures, or simply enjoy a cup of coffee, I hope this guide helps you discover a new favorite spot.

Why I Made This Guide

Coffee shops can become more than just places to buy drinks. They can become places where we study, talk, think, create, and make memories. This guide is my small collection of those places around Pasay, shared one coffee corner at a time.
